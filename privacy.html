The important details

Privacy policy.

Last updated October 21, 2026

This policy explains how Atelier handles information when you visit this website or place an order. It applies to information collected through this storefront.

Information you provide

If you check out, payment and order information you enter is collected through Stripe’s hosted checkout. The information requested can include your name, email address, delivery address, and payment details. Your payment card details are entered directly with Stripe and are not stored by this website.

Information used by this website

Your shopping bag is stored in your browser’s local storage so it remains available as you move between shop pages. It contains product identifiers and quantities; clear your browser storage to remove it. The shop’s hosting and security services may process technical information such as your IP address, browser details, and requests to deliver and protect the website.

How information is used

Information is used to operate the storefront, create and support orders, provide customer service, protect the site against abuse, and meet applicable legal obligations. We do not sell your personal information.

Service providers

Stripe processes checkout and payment information under its own privacy policy and terms. The website’s hosting provider processes information needed to deliver the site. These providers may handle information in accordance with their own policies and applicable law.

Retention and your choices

Order-related information may be retained for as long as needed to complete transactions, provide support, and meet legal and accounting requirements. You can clear the shopping bag from your browser at any time. For questions or requests about information associated with an order, contact the store using the contact details supplied with your order.

Children and policy updates

This shop is intended for general audiences and is not directed to children under 13. This policy may be updated from time to time; the date above indicates when it was last revised.

Stripe’s privacy information is available at stripe.com/privacy.
